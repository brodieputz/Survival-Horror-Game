DREAD DEPTHS

The last train out. How many nights can your camp hold?

Your camp is pitched beside a stalled train. By day, scavenge the buildings around you, recruit survivors, arm them, build up the barricade and mount turrets on the train cars. By night, the dead may come. When you have enough coal, move the train on. You only get one life.

Walkers & Runners

The common dead. Walkers shamble, runners sprint. Later hordes bring bloaters, rotters and riot-armoured zombies.

Blood Hound

Its shriek whips the whole horde into a frenzy. Hiding spots won't fool one on your trail.

Blind Brute

Hunts by sound and tears through barricades. Shoot it and it charges.

Angel
