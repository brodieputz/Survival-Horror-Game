DREAD DEPTHS

The last train out. How many nights can your camp hold?

Your camp is pitched beside a stalled train. By day, scavenge the buildings around you, recruit survivors, arm them, build up the barricade and mount turrets on the train cars. By night, the dead may come. When you have enough coal, move the train on. Save whenever you like; choose for each run whether death is final.
